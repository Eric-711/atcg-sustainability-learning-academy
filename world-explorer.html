🎓 Your mission

Investigate latitude, longitude, hemispheres, climate zones, geography, historic sites, human impacts, and global change. Challenges are generated from a rotating destination library and question templates for replayability.

Historical facts describe the past; current-event questions are general learning themes, not live news. Future-event questions are explicitly hypothetical scenarios.

Educational map is stylized and not intended for precise navigation. Points are approximate. Google Earth is a separate Google service; ATCG does not control its availability or content. Your XP is stored locally on this device.
